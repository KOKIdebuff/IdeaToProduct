## 2. Planning impact

- New `P0-04-T18` is required before the HTML Builder: Kernel-owned
  state-version Fact bindings plus typed `report_projection` materialization.
- `P0-04-T11` must depend on T18 and only builds/publishes the Report; it does
  not render SVG/PNG, research facts, or score competitors.
- Unfinished `P0-04-T10` becomes a planned Transparent Scoring task family.
  Its Profile Rubric Catalogue and governance are approved; implementation
  remains absent until the complete `0.3.1` Bundle and later Scoring tasks run.
- `P0-04-T12` remains a later Verifier task and may only publish a constrained
  Verification Summary successor after its own Contract/implementation work.
- `P0-04-T17` has completed its isolated native SVG core, but remains
  in progress until the later Runtime path emits verified chart collections,
  contained Assets, PNG compatibility state and recovery/invalidation evidence.

## 3. Required future evidence

Before `0.3.1` may be registered or used for a new Run, future work must
provide active-Run-safe Resolver/Registry support and end-to-end Runtime
integration/tests for Citation Closure, local Report Bundle publication, CAS
successors, Fact Provenance, chart collection/PNG compatibility, scoring,
compatibility, and exact resume/audit behavior.

This document records the isolated Renderer-core completion but not active-Run
or end-to-end Runtime verification. It does not authorize a partial Bundle, a
Registry default change, cross-version fallback, migration of existing `0.3.0`
Runs, or mutation of historical hashes.
